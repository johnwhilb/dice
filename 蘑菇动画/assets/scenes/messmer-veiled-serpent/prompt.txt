Design an original exclusive boss arena for Messmer the Impaler: "Chamber of the Veiled Serpent". A single finished 2D game environment background, no text, no border, no UI, no characters. Reference 1 is the BATTLE BACKGROUND STYLE reference: match clean bold dark contours, broad flat colors, restrained two-tone cel shadows, angular simplified architecture and sparse hand-drawn stone marks. Avoid painterly texture, photorealism, complex rendering and excessive detail. Reference 2 is Messmer's master character sheet: translate his dark bronze armor, winged snake crown, crimson drapes, slender spear and coiling serpent shapes into environmental architecture ONLY. Do not draw Messmer or living snakes.

Art direction: a majestic secluded sanctum inside a shadow fortress. At the extreme left and right, tall dark bronze winged-serpent carved buttresses and pointed spear-tip finials frame the view, with a few small amber ritual flames and wine-red banners. Across the upper background, a layered gallery of weathered recessed arches and monumental dark hanging curtains creates architectural depth. Some side arches reveal a smoky dim inner cloister. Central distant architecture is visible and meaningful but subdued: a broad shallow recessed sanctuary with muted low-relief serpent interlace and sweeping shadow shapes; no central glowing door or bright throne. Rich environmental scenery can exist behind the enemy; control value, contrast and edge weight to keep it subordinate. Palette: charcoal, smoky warm stone, dark muted bronze, subdued deep crimson on the edges. Avoid a large red background mass behind the red-haired red-cloaked boss. Standing area: a broad weathered ceremonial stone arena, with a subtle broken circular bronze inlay and sparse soot marks, all flush with the surface. The inlay is quiet, not a bright focal symbol. No stairs, pits or obstacles in the standing space. Make this a coherent lived-in fortress sanctuary rather than a generic flat empty wall.

COMPOSITION AND CAMERA — HIGHEST PRIORITY
Create a complete panoramic battle background at 1750 × 750 pixels.
Preserve the fixed framing and visual center. Exclude characters and UI;
the enemy will be composited separately.

HORIZONTAL LAYOUT: left 15% / center 70% / right 15%.
Use the outer regions for balanced environmental framing. Keep the central
70% spacious and readable, without large foreground obstructions.

VERTICAL LAYOUT: upper 48% environmental background / lower 52% enemy
standing area. These describe visual functions, not a mandatory wall and floor.
Use terrain, architecture, vegetation, distant scenery or other suitable elements.
Keep the depth transition near Y 48%, connected naturally across the image.

The background may contain rich scenery and recognizable objects. Control
their contrast, brightness, edges and detail so they support the environment
without competing with the enemy. Place stronger accents toward the sides;
avoid bright focal points or dominant lines behind the expected silhouette.

Reserve enemy placement at X 50%, spanning approximately X 30–70%, with
ground contact at Y 88–92%. Provide a believable standing surface and clear
silhouette space. Keep the bottom center unobstructed while continuing natural
textures and environmental detail throughout; avoid an artificial blank zone.

CAMERA: centered first-person view, eye level, looking down 4–6 degrees,
zero roll and no horizontal offset. Use mild perspective depth, with horizon
and central vanishing point at X 50%, Y 48%.
